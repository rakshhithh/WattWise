03 · Forecasting Models

Long Short-Term Memory network captures nonlinear temporal dependencies via a 48-step sliding window. First 47 time steps serve as input; 48th step is the prediction target. Produces rolling 48-hour forecast.

04 · Dynamic Ensemble
⚖️
Error-Based Ensemble Strategy
Dynamic Weighted Ensemble Model — yE(t)
Combines LSTM and Prophet forecasts using inverse MAE weighting from the validation set. Automatically prioritizes the model with superior recent performance. Balances statistical interpretability with nonlinear learning capacity.
yE(t) = wP · yP(t) + wL · yL(t) subject to: wP + wL = 1
wL = (1/MAE_L) / (1/MAE_L + 1/MAE_P) , wP = 1 − wL
